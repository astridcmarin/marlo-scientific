Scope, grouping and source definitions

Method: {{ summary.method_version }}. All counts use this completed cohort's frozen membership and exact retained snapshots.

{{ summary.grouping_rule }} Group counts are deduplicated study counts. Each study has one reported stopping field and contributes to one text group when present.

TERMINATED is the default scope. WITHDRAWN and SUSPENDED are separate statuses; ALL combines only these three. COMPLETED, UNKNOWN and other statuses are excluded even if their record contains stopping text. Missing or malformed status is not treated as termination.

Missing means an absent, null or blank string. A nonstring value is invalid. Nonblank placeholders such as “N/A” or “Unknown” remain reported text rather than being assigned a clinical meaning. Capitalization/whitespace grouping does not interpret negation or infer causes.
