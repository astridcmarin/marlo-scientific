Method and limits
  • Source: registered eligibility criteria of {{ "{:,}".format(data.trials|length) }} multiple myeloma interventional trials (ClinicalTrials.gov, retrieved 5 October 2026; corpus content SHA256 {{ data.corpus_content_sha256[:12] }}…).
  • Reader: {{ data.extractor_version }} (SHA256 {{ data.extractor_sha256[:12] }}…), rule-based; frozen before its accuracy check.
  • Values are counted as the condition for eligibility; exclusion criteria are turned into the eligible side. Exceptions for some patients (for example, marrow involvement or Gilbert's syndrome) are not counted as separate thresholds.
  • Units are grouped only by exact factors: /µL, cells/µL, × 10⁹/L and K/µL; g/dL and g/L; mg/dL and µmol/L. × ULN, creatinine clearance (mL/min) and eGFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) stay separate.
  • A trial with several values (cohorts or parts) appears under each value; the draft comparison uses its first value.
  • Descriptive only: it does not recommend criteria or judge any protocol.
