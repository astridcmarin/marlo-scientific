Explore the hypothesis

Set the coupling gain to 0: binding still changes, but the downstream amount stays at baseline. Set free concentration to 0: neither system binds and both downstream amounts stay at baseline.

With a positive gain, bound fraction continues to drive the assumed loss after free concentration switches off. Amount changes continuously. Its recovery depends on both the remaining binding and its own turnover rate; it is not forced to follow occupancy instantaneously.

Binding is calculated analytically. Downstream amount uses numerical RK4 integration with a maximum step of 0.01 h and exact pulse/sample boundaries. Smooth plotted lines connect sampled values; they are not measurements or uncertainty intervals.
