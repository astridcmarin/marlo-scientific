How the reference works

The supported structure uses eight serial absorption-delay boxes feeding one central compartment. The analytic reference computes each requested amount's contribution and adds the contributions at each archived time. Its integrated AUC follows the corresponding elimination balance.

This audit does not independently recheck source transcription or covariate-to-parameter derivation; the reference uses the archived clearance, volume and transit rate.

Units, constant parameters, zero random effects and initial amounts follow the archived calculation's assumptions. This reference checks those equations rather than introducing physiology, uncertainty or observed clinical measurements. Unsupported archived versions or rates withhold the analytic audit.
