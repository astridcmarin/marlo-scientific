Comparison rules and provenance

Only reported year bounds are compared; numerical boundaries are inclusive. Explicit N/A describes no numerical limit reported in that field. Missing, blank or malformed values are not assumed unlimited. Non-year units are retained without approximate conversion. A known year-bound violation can establish an outside-field result despite the other bound being unknown. An inverted reported year range remains unresolved.

Missing synthetic age or UNSPECIFIED sex remains unresolved for that dimension. The sex field accepts ALL, FEMALE and MALE. Gender criteria and all other eligibility text require separate review. Weight, eGFR, dose and medication names are not used by this comparison.
