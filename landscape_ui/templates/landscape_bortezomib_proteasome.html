What the equation means

Activity = 100 − 71.1 × C^1.47 / (1.79^1.47 + C^1.47), with C in nM.

At zero concentration, activity is 100%. At 1.79 nM, inhibition reaches half its model maximum and activity is 64.45%. At very high concentrations, activity approaches 28.9%. These are mathematical properties, not individual-patient guarantees.

Proteasome activity is an endpoint distinct from target occupancy. This component has no time delay or memory. Connecting it to a concentration-time model would require a compatible bortezomib PK model and a separate validation step.
