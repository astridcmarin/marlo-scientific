Why the curves overlap

Some amount from an earlier addition remains when the next addition arrives. Because these equations are linear and their parameters stay constant, the response to each addition can be shifted in time and summed. This is called superposition.

The example retains volume 10 L, clearance 2 L/h, transit rate 1/h and three mathematical delay stages. The stages are not organs. Three additions do not establish steady state, and the method does not model saturation or changing physiology.
