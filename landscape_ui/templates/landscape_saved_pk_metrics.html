How to interpret the peak

Cmax describes maximum concentration and Tmax its timing under a defined profile and observation window. This summary reports only the highest archived sample and its time. A continuous peak can fall between samples, so these values are not presented as exact continuous Cmax or Tmax.

Repeated administration can produce multiple peaks. This summary selects the overall sampled maximum in the recorded window, rather than a per-dose or steady-state peak. The time is relative to the window start, not the most recent administration.

A mathematical audit must pass before metrics are shown. It verifies agreement with supported equations using archived parameters; it does not establish patient applicability or clinical efficacy, toxicity, uncertainty or dose suitability.
