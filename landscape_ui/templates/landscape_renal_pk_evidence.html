How to interpret this reference

AUC summarizes exposure over time. Here AUC∞ includes extrapolation beyond the sampling period. It is not the same endpoint as a saved AUC from 0 to 24 hours.

The ratio column divides each group's reported arithmetic mean AUC by the normal group's mean. It is not an individual exposure ratio, an adjusted causal effect or a comparison with your model. No confidence interval was calculated; SD describes dispersion.
