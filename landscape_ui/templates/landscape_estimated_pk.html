What the reference ratio means

The reference is the mean AUC measured after a single 25 mg dose, the approved full starting dose, in 7 subjects with normal renal function in study CC-5013-PK-001 (2181 ± 703 ng·h/mL). Exposure is dose-proportional, so a single-dose AUC equals the AUC of one day on daily dosing, and the ratio compares like with like. Both models reproduce this reference for normal renal function. The approved renal dose reductions were described as aiming for daily exposure comparable to the full starting dose with normal renal function; the ratio shows where this synthetic patient's typical value sits relative to that measurement.

It is not a target, limit, toxicity prediction or dose recommendation. The ratio uses typical values; real patients with these covariates vary widely around it, and the reference itself comes from 7 subjects.
