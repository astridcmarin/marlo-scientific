Lenalidomide is not metabolised by liver enzymes; most of a dose leaves unchanged in urine, by filtration of unbound drug and by active secretion from the kidney tubules. This page shows how a model calibrated on people with normal kidney function divides elimination between those routes at this record's estimated kidney function.

How to read this

  • Filtration is the unbound fraction of drug (77%) times the filtration rate. Secretion is active transport by the tubules; with normal kidney function it is almost half of renal clearance, which is why renal clearance exceeds what filtration alone could achieve.
  • As kidney function falls, both routes shrink, and the small non-renal route, assumed constant, takes a larger share. Less of the dose then leaves in urine, and more of it stays in the body for longer.
