What to look for

Try strength 1: both curves stay at baseline. Increase strength: both pulse equilibria fall, while increased loss also accelerates the approach to its equilibrium. Change pulse duration to see whether either system gets close before the pulse ends.

Once both processes return to baseline, their recovery relaxation rate is the same, although they start recovery at different amounts. A protein amount at one time does not by itself identify which mechanism caused it. Real mechanism assessment requires appropriate experimental evidence.
