Not dosing guidance

For one synthetic record, this page shows the typical daily exposure the two published models give at each of their own study doses, inside each model's scope, together with the relative odds of grade 3/4 cytopenia from the published exposure–safety analysis. No dose is recommended or ranked. There is no exposure–efficacy evidence on the platform, so a lower exposure here is not a better dose.

Relative odds of grade 3/4 cytopenia against the reference exposure

From the sponsor's exposure–safety analysis in myeloma (lenalidomide monotherapy, as reported by the Australian TGA): relative odds = (exposure ratio)ln(odds ratio), for a patient with the same disease and baseline count. Values are the relative odds, with the 95% interval carried from the published odds ratio, and the middle 90% across patients from the model's between-patient variability in clearance.
