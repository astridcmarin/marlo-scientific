25 findings: 1 high, 8 medium, 16 low. The arithmetic is sound: every locked report reproduces exactly, and every transcription that could be checked against a PDF is correct. The weaknesses are in interpretation: what the ratio endpoint hides, what "blind" and "consistent" mean, and self-scored coverage.

The Hughes 2019 article and supplement are not in the supplied materials and could not be fetched, so scope item 1 (Hughes transcription, the Cockcroft–Gault wording, the precision extract) is **not verified against the paper**. ## Checked, no issue

- **Reproducibility.** All ten reports regenerate identically from the code, including `report_sha256`: Hughes and Kim renal-group checks, the recheck, the Bridoux check, pathway checks v1–v3, the end-stage check, and time-course checks v1 and v2.
- **Documented numbers.** The tables in `PATIENT_LAB_2_0.md` (2.0g, 2.1c, 2.1e, 2.1h), the R5 and R9 figures in the checklist, and the parameter-uncertainty example (2,370–3,015 ng·h/mL; peak 336–391 ng/mL) match recomputation.
- **Kim 2026 transcription.** Table 3 (all estimates, %RSE, bootstrap intervals, IIV, residual error), Table 1 ranges and medians, Table 2 group CL/F, sampling times, Du Bois and CKD-EPI 2021 statements, and the page-10 equations (including V2/F printed 21.1 vs Table 3 21.2) all match the PDF. PDF SHA-256 matches the pin.
- **Bridoux 2016 transcription.** Table 1 and Table 2 values, statistic, footnote (AUC48/2), doses and sampling match. CL/F and AUC24 are mutually consistent in every group. PDF SHA-256 matches.
- **EMA II-0056 transcription.** Tables 3, 4 and 7, the Figure 1 regressions and the dialysis text match the rendered pages 18–21. PDF SHA-256 matches.
- **No leak of Bridoux values through the EMA reports.** Neither EMA report (II-0056, II-0107) contains Bridoux's per-group results; reading II-0056 after the plan lock did not expose them.
- **Welch intervals.** The formula is right for geometric means (log SD = √ln(1 + GCV²), Welch SE and df, t quantile) and labelled as approximate and platform-derived. Hand check of the moderate ratio: 0.87–1.22.
- **Relative-odds algebra.** (AUC ratio)^ln(OR), interval limits swapped below 1, between-patient range = ln(OR) × ω. Correct as mathematics.
- **Unbound-to-total conversion for Kim.** CL and volumes multiplied by Fu; agrees with Table 2 (9.0 × 0.614 ≈ 5.5 L/h).
- **Population band.** Joint draw of (η_CL, η_V) by Cholesky, η_KTR independent; eight first-order stages for "7 transit compartments" plus the absorption depot; exact solution cross-checked against the numerical curve on every run.
- **Hughes numbers reachable from the abstract.** CL/F 12 (10.8–13.6), Vd/F 68.8 (61.8–76.3), KTR 13.5 (11.9–36.8), 125 subjects, 2.5–75 mg, and "22% lower clearance" = (30/90)^0.224.
- **Measured vs Cockcroft–Gault grouping.** The mismatch in the first two plans is disclosed and corrected in `SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md`; its direction would deepen, not remove, the Hughes under-prediction.
- **Units.** mL/min to L/h, µmol/L to mg/dL and ng·h/mL conversions are explicit and correct where read. No silent default found in the Hughes weight basis or Kim equation form (both explicit arguments; the dose view states its fixed choices).
- **Honest wording.** The exposure–safety MAR, the time-course result text and the dataset ledger's not-blind roles state their limits plainly. Unit tests that do not need the web app pass (parameter uncertainty, EMA 2013, time course, ledger, and the non-page tests of dose–exposure, relative odds, kidney, dialysis and hepatic views).
