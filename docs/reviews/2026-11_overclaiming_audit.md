## Consistent and fine (reuse these)

- **Result banner** (`run_record_macros.html`, `simulation_banner`): chips "Simulated · Synthetic patient record · Not a clinical prediction", one sentence saying what the result is, a list of what "It is not", then "Clinical prediction validated: false · Patient simulation ready: false".
- **Result-type "is / is not" text** (`run_record.py`, `RESULT_TYPES`), for example "a recommendation to give, hold or change a dose" under "is not".
- **Scope panel**: "outside the model's scope — no model value is computed"; "Flagged: a locked outside check found this model inconsistent for records like this one … read it with the observed data below."
- **Refusing to compute**: "Unknown stays unknown", "not a study dose", "Outside a reported range means extrapolation from the source study, not a clinical threshold."
- **Relative odds**: "Relative, not absolute … They are not probabilities: the baseline risk was not published"; "Two different uncertainties."
- **Agreement is not validation**: "The model was fitted to these data, so agreement here is consistency, not validation."; "A consistent result means the model agreed with that data under the plan's rule; it is not a clinical validation."; "A checksum does not authenticate a source or validate a model."
- **Blindness labels** (`check_findings.py`): "Not blind: data seen before the plan"; "Prespecified, with a recorded deviation; outcome largely known beforehand".
- **AI labelling where present**: "(an AI review, not independent)"; "Extracted with AI assistance; not reviewed by the platform owner."; report `not_this`: "the narrative sections were written by the AI assistant and have not been reviewed independently."
- **Dose view body**: "No dose is recommended or ranked. There is no exposure–efficacy evidence on the platform, so a lower exposure here is not a better dose."; "shown, not used".
- **Label curves**: "Typical value from the label — not a population model, not a prediction for any patient"; "a comparison of two models — weaker than agreement with data".
- **Scorecards**: "Where outside data disagree" placed above the checks; "Never claimed" and "Not established" sections.
- **Intended-use page**: the "Not intended for" list and "No model on the platform is validated for clinical prediction."
- **Findings file v3 headlines**: plain, failures included, with numbers.
