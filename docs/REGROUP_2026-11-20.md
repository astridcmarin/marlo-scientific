## Assessment

**Strong.**
- Plans locked before computation, a ledger of what has been seen, and published failures. It held under pressure: the NCT01801436 result was locked, reproducible and pinned, even with two sessions working at once.
- The evidence layer is coherent: frozen cohorts, special-population evidence for six drugs, each drug's sources and regulatory documents, and every check linked from its drug page.
- The eligibility reference is now shown adequate, which unblocks extractor work.

**Weak, each with its fix.**
1. **The first release has no pipeline.** Decision 0007 commits to every drug with a US label; its five choices are open, and the working sessions cannot download in bulk. Today there are ten hand-built drugs. *Fix:* the owner's five choices, a storage and ingestion decision record, then a pilot of about 50 labels with sampled review.
2. **The models are not a product feature.** Only the lenalidomide models are reasonably supported; bortezomib now fails blind as well as unblinded; carfilzomib has no curve. *Fix:* keep models behind their records; no new model pages before a model passes a blind check.
3. **No independent review** since the 18 November decision to include one. *Fix:* one reviewer for the lenalidomide model and its credibility record.
4. **Two sessions wrote to one branch** on 20 November (checkpoint finding 1). *Fix:* fetch and check the branch head before each commit; a branch per session (`CLAUDE.md`, this batch).
5. **The documents had drifted:** the README and the roadmap number their phases differently; the roadmap carried 5 and 18 November counts; the handoff was dated 16 November; seven step-numbered documents (STEP104–109) sat at the top of `docs/`. *Fix:* this batch.
