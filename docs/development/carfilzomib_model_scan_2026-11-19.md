# Carfilzomib: published population PK models (scan of 19 November 2026)

After the label curve was not eligible (finding `carfilzomib_label`), a short web search for published carfilzomib population PK models. Search results only; no paper has been read. Nothing here is a parameter value.

| Candidate | What the search shows | Fit for a blind check against Badros 2013 and Quach 2017 |
|---|---|---|
| **Ou Y, Doshi S, Nguyen A, et al. Population pharmacokinetics and exposure–response relationship of carfilzomib in patients with multiple myeloma. J Clin Pharmacol 2017;57(5):663–677. doi:10.1002/jcph.850** | Myeloma; pooled from five phase 1b/2 and two phase 3 studies; twice-weekly regimens 15 to 20/56 mg/m²; body surface area on clearance the only significant covariate. | The best candidate, with one condition to settle first: if its pooled phase 1b/2 studies include Badros 2013's renal-impairment study, Badros is its fitting data, not outside data, and only Quach 2017 could test it. The study list is in the paper's methods. |
| Two-compartment population PK/PD model in relapsed or refractory DLBCL with R-ICE (Target Oncol 2023, doi:10.1007/s11523-023-00992-4) | 28 patients; two compartments; typical clearance reported in the abstract. | Weak: lymphoma, combination chemotherapy, small. |
| Mechanistic PK/PD model of proteasome inhibition for longer dosing intervals (Clin Pharmacokinet 2023, doi:10.1007/s40262-023-01242-6) | Bottom-up model of proteasome inhibition; PK described from published information. | Not a population PK model fitted to patient data; not suited to an exposure check. |

**Blinding.** The search returned a snippet of Quach 2017's abstract Results and Conclusion (a trend toward higher AUC and Cmax in ESRD; no meaningful PK differences).
