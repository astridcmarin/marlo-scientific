Substantial functionality

Clinical trial intelligence

Search, filter and analyze clinical trial and regulatory intelligence from ClinicalTrials.gov.

  • Search and filter by condition, intervention, sponsor and location; narrow by status, phase, study type and sex; sort by recency or enrollment.
  • Full study records, organized: identification, sponsor and leadership, design, arms and interventions, dates, eligibility, locations, contacts and outcomes.
  • Regulatory overview for each study: FDA-regulated drug or device, data monitoring committee, FDAAA 801 and results-reporting indicators, expanded access, and the registration-to-results timeline.
  • Trial and regulatory analytics across the results: phase, status, sponsor class, top sponsors, intervention types, countries, enrollment and study types.
  • Research layer: drug names and combination regimens normalized to consistent identities, trials grouped into cohorts and compared, evidence saved and summarized.

What it can do today ↓

Patient Lab and Simulation Lab

See how a drug behaves across different patients

Build a synthetic patient with age, sex, body size, kidney function and laboratory values, then run published models on that record. Each result is a typical value from a published model, never a prediction for a real person.

Synthetic patient records

Structured patient states with saved scenarios, revision history and side-by-side comparisons. Every value records where it came from.

Published PK models

Lenalidomide concentration–time curves from two population PK models (Hughes 2019, Kim 2026), with covariates estimated from the patient record and between-patient variability shown.

Kidney elimination pathways

How lenalidomide clearance splits between filtration, tubular secretion and non-renal routes as kidney function falls, checked against a published renal-impairment study.

Exposure and safety signals

Relative odds of severe low blood counts at a patient's simulated exposure, from a published exposure–safety analysis. Relative, not absolute, and labelled that way.

Pharmacodynamic time courses

The typical platelet course under bortezomib from a published model (Lignet 2022), scaled to the patient's starting count and graded against CTCAE v5.0.

Mechanism building blocks

Modules for target occupancy, binding kinetics, protein turnover, transit delays, treatment regimens and parameter sensitivity.

Provenance first

Every result shows its evidence, including where a model falls short

A simulated number is only as useful as what you know about it. Marlo Scientific records where each value came from and what has, and has not, been checked.

  • Pinned sources

    Reference data is checksum-pinned and versioned. A changed file is refused, and updates are new versions rather than silent edits.

  • Four validation layers

    Software verification, transcription against the source, reproduction of published results, and consistency with outside data, reported separately for every model.

  • Checks planned before they run

    Comparisons with outside data follow a plan fixed in advance, and the results are kept whichever way they go, including when they contradict a model.

  • Unknown stays unknown

    No silent defaults or unit conversions. Assumptions are named, with their status: sourced, assumed, unresolved or contradicted.
